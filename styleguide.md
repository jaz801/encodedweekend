# ENCODED — Brand Guide

*Derived from the live site at [encoded.ai](https://www.encoded.ai/). Colors and type were pulled from the site's computed styles; the typeface specimen was provided by the brand.*

---

## 1. Brand at a glance

ENCODED is a science-backed nervous-system / "frequency" training protocol — daily handwriting routines plus an AI-personalized plan that rewires subconscious neural pathways for higher performance, clearer thinking, and emotional mastery.

The brand reads as **premium, calm, and clinical-but-human**: a near-black canvas, a single warm champagne-gold accent, generous negative space, and warm lifestyle photography (journals, coffee, handwriting). It feels closer to a high-end performance/wellness product than a typical SaaS app — confident, restrained, and a little cinematic.

**Three words:** Premium · Grounded · Precise.

---

## 2. Logo

The mark pairs a small **audio-waveform / equalizer-bar icon** with the **ENCODED** wordmark set in clean white letterforms with light tracking. The waveform nods to "frequency"; the wordmark is set in all caps with relaxed spacing.

Usage notes:

- Always present on the dark canvas — white mark on near-black is the default lockup.
- Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the "E".
- Don't recolor the wordmark; the gold accent is reserved for icons, labels, and buttons, not the logotype.

### Gradients (used for depth, not decoration)

The site layers very subtle dark gradients to fade sections into one another and create a soft "spotlight" behind hero content:

- **Section fade:** `linear-gradient(#141414, rgba(20,20,20,0) 0%, rgba(20,20,20,0.95) 87%)`
- **Top spotlight:** `radial-gradient(circle closest-corner at 50% 0, #222222, #141414 55%)`
- **Card sheen:** `linear-gradient(#242424, #1B1B1B)`

> **Rule of thumb:** background and structure are always neutral dark; gold is an accent that should occupy roughly 5–10% of any given screen.

## 4. Typography

Four typefaces, each with a clear job. The hierarchy is: **Switzer** for display, **Manrope** for everything functional, **Space Mono** for data, **Volkart** as an editorial serif accent.

### Switzer — Display & headings
- **Weights in use:** Medium (500), Semibold (600)
- **Where:** All H1–H3 headlines (e.g. hero "Train For…", "Map. Train. Expand.")
- **Settings:** Large sizes (≈32–68px), tight tracking on big sizes (≈ -1px letter-spacing), mostly sentence case / capitalize. Never all-caps.
- **Feel:** Modern geometric-humanist sans; confident but soft.

### Manrope — Body, UI & eyebrow labels
- **Weights in use:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600)
- **Where:** Body paragraphs (~15px, Medium, slight 0.6px tracking), nav, buttons, and the small **uppercase gold eyebrow labels** above each section ("WHAT IT IS", "PERSONALIZED", "EVIDENCE" — ~13px, uppercase, letter-spaced, gold `#C4A46C`).
- **Feel:** Clean, neutral, highly legible workhorse.

### Space Mono — Data & big numbers
- **Weight:** Regular (400)
- **Where:** Oversized statistics ("90%+" at ~96px) and any technical/numeric callouts.
- **Feel:** Monospaced, "instrument readout" — reinforces the measurable, science-backed positioning.

### Volkart — Editorial serif accent
- **Weight:** Regular
- **Where:** Reserved for editorial / quote / "human" moments and printed Anchor Journal touches. Use sparingly as a contrast voice against the sans system.
- **Feel:** Warm contemporary serif; adds soul and a tactile, journal-like quality.

### Type pairing rules

- Headlines: **Switzer Medium**, white, tight tracking, sentence case.
- Eyebrow labels: **Manrope**, uppercase, ~13px, letter-spaced, gold.
- Body: **Manrope Medium**, ~15px, color `#D0D0D0`, comfortable line height.
- Stats / numbers: **Space Mono Regular**, large, white or gold.
- Editorial accent: **Volkart Regular**, sparing.

## 5. Look & feel

**Layout.** Roomy, centered, single-column storytelling broken up by three-up card grids. Lots of dark negative space between sections; content arrives in calm, deliberate beats rather than dense screens.

**Cards & surfaces.** Rounded corners (~8px on buttons, larger soft radii on cards), faint `#272727` borders, subtle dark-on-dark gradients for depth. Surfaces feel matte and tactile, never glossy.

**Buttons.** Primary CTA ("Start Training") is solid **button gold** with near-black text and a pill/rounded shape. Secondary actions are ghost/transparent with light-gray text. Nav links are quiet gray, brightening on hover.

**Photography.** Warm, intimate lifestyle imagery — a journal and pen, coffee, hands writing, soft natural light. The warm tones in the photos echo the gold accent and keep the dark UI from feeling cold. Imagery is real and human, not abstract or stocky.

**Iconography & motifs.** The waveform/frequency motif (equalizer bars) is the signature graphic device. Keep icons thin-stroke, monochrome or gold.

**Motion (implied).** Soft fades and gentle spotlight gradients suggest calm, premium transitions — nothing snappy or playful.

**Overall mood.** Elite performance training meets quiet luxury. Dark, focused, and measured, with one warm gold thread tying it together. It should feel like something you'd trust with your mind.

---

## 6. Voice & tone

The copy is **direct, confident, and benefit-led**, with short declarative sentences and frequent two- or three-beat rhythms.

- **Cadence:** Short, punchy fragments. "Map. Train. Expand." / "Powerful Results. Within Weeks." / "Other Methods Manage Symptoms. ENCODED Upgrades the Root Cause."
- **Authority through evidence:** Leans on science ("Backed by 100+ peer-reviewed studies", "neuroscience-backed") without jargon overload.
- **Aspirational but grounded:** Promises transformation ("What used to feel like your ceiling becomes your floor") while staying concrete ("15 to 25 min/day", "Shifts within 10 days").
- **Second person:** Speaks directly to "you" and "your life, your goals, your vision."
- **Brand name:** Written **ENCODED** in all caps in body copy.

Avoid: hype-y exclamation, woo-woo mysticism without the science framing, dense paragraphs, and cold corporate jargon.
